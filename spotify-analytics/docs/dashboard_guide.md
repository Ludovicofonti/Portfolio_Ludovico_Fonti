# Looker Studio Dashboard Guide

[**Open the shared Spotify Italy Analytics report →**](https://datastudio.google.com/s/lDpEXFyep90)

This guide explains the business purpose and field configuration of every unique
visualization currently shown in the report. The report is the canonical presentation
layer; GitHub links to it because interactive Looker Studio reports cannot be embedded
in GitHub Markdown. Anonymous sessions currently reach Google sign-in, so recruiter
access must be enabled in the report's sharing settings.

## Global report controls

- Default country: `IT`.
- Default date range: all available observed history.
- All percentage fields use percentage formatting with two decimal places.
- Artist and track charts should expose the entity name, current value and date in the
  tooltip.
- Dates must sort ascending in time-series charts.
- Missing observations must remain gaps. Never replace a missing day with zero streams.
- Charts described as “latest snapshot” must filter `chart_date` to the maximum
  available date.

## Page 1 — Market overview

The first page answers: **How large is the observed Spotify Italy Top 200 market, how
concentrated is it, and how much demand comes from new versus established catalogue?**

### Executive scorecards

All scorecards use `spotify_analytics_marts.rpt_market_overview_daily`.

| Scorecard | Business question | Looker Studio field | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ultimo Aggiornamento** | How current is the report? | `MAX(chart_date)` | Latest successfully observed chart date. It is the first freshness check before interpreting any KPI. |
| **Stream Giornalieri Medi** | What is the normal daily demand captured by the Top 200? | `AVG(streams)` | Average total streams per observed day in the selected period. It establishes the market baseline. |
| **Variazione Giornaliera Stream** | Is the market gaining or losing daily consumption? | `AVG(streams_change)` | Mean day-over-day change in total Top 200 streams. Positive values indicate expansion; negative values indicate contraction. |
| **Quota Stream Top 10** | How much demand is controlled by the ten largest tracks? | `AVG(top_10_stream_share)` | Average share of Top 200 streams generated by ranks 1–10. A rise means attention is concentrating at the top. |
| **Quota Stream Top 50** | How deep is meaningful demand beyond the very top? | `AVG(top_50_stream_share)` | Average share generated by ranks 1–50. Compare it with Top 10 share to distinguish superstar concentration from broader hit depth. |
| **Quota Collaborazioni** | How important are multi-artist releases? | `AVG(collaboration_share)` | Share of charted tracks credited to more than one artist. It measures catalogue format, not stream share. |
| **Quota Explicit** | How prevalent is explicit content in the chart? | `AVG(explicit_share)` | Share of charted tracks flagged explicit by Spotify metadata. It supports audience and editorial positioning decisions. |

The current report layout contains two identical **Variazione Giornaliera Stream**
cards. Only one should remain; this guide treats it as one KPI.

### Stream Top 200 nel Tempo

- **Chart type:** time series with area fill.
- **Source:** `rpt_market_overview_daily`.
- **Dimension:** `chart_date`.
- **Metric:** `SUM(streams)`.
- **Sort:** `chart_date` ascending.
- **Recommended validity filter:** `track_count >= 190` and `streams > 0`.

**Business question:** Is total demand for the Spotify Italy Top 200 growing, stable or
contracting over time?

**Meaning:** each point is the combined stream count of the observed Top 200 for one
day. Sustained changes indicate a market-level shift; isolated peaks can reflect major
releases or calendar effects. A value of zero is a collection/coverage issue, not a real
collapse in listening, and must be filtered or shown as a gap.

### Nuove Uscite vs Catalogo Consolidato

- **Chart type:** 100% stacked column chart.
- **Source:** `rpt_market_overview_daily`.
- **Dimension:** `chart_date`.
- **Metrics:** `SUM(fresh_streams)`, `SUM(developing_streams)`,
  `SUM(catalog_streams)`.
- **Series labels:** “Release fino a 60 giorni”, “Release da 61 a 180 giorni”,
  “Release oltre 180 giorni”.
- **Sort:** `chart_date` ascending.

**Business question:** Is current demand driven by recent releases or by established
catalogue?

**Meaning:** every bar divides that day's Top 200 streams into three release-age
segments. A growing fresh share suggests a release-led market; a growing catalogue
share signals durable, evergreen consumption. The chart should use the three metrics
directly—without an “Other” dimension bucket—so every date totals 100%.

## Page 2 — Artist and track dynamics

The second page answers: **Which artists command attention, whether that advantage
comes from one hit or catalogue breadth, and which tracks combine longevity with
current intensity?**

### Top 15 Artisti per Stream

- **Chart type:** horizontal bar chart.
- **Source:** `rpt_artist_performance_daily`.
- **Dimension:** `artist_name`.
- **Metric:** `SUM(streams)`.
- **Row limit:** 15.
- **Sort:** `SUM(streams)` descending.
- **Date behavior:** aggregate across the selected report period.

**Business question:** Which artists generated the most chart streams in the selected
period?

**Meaning:** the ranking measures accumulated demand, not only peak position. In the
shared screenshot, Geolier leads with about 83.4 million streams, followed by Shiva and
Sfera Ebbasta. Because the metric sums across the selected dates, a durable catalogue
can outperform a short-lived spike.

### Ampiezza Catalogo vs Quota di Mercato

- **Chart type:** bubble chart.
- **Source:** `rpt_artist_performance_daily`.
- **Dimension:** `artist_name`.
- **X-axis:** `MAX(track_count)`.
- **Y-axis:** `MAX(market_share)`.
- **Bubble size:** `MAX(streams)`.
- **Colour dimension:** `artist_segment` (`Top 5`, `Top 15`, `Long tail`).
- **Date behavior:** latest snapshot only.

**Business question:** Is an artist's market share supported by catalogue breadth or by
a small number of dominant tracks?

**Meaning:** points in the upper-right combine many charting tracks with high share and
therefore represent broad catalogue strength. Upper-left points indicate hit
concentration: strong share with few tracks. Lower-right points have broad presence but
limited efficiency per track. Bubble size makes the absolute stream contribution
visible alongside percentage share.

### Quota di Mercato degli Artisti nel Tempo

- **Chart type:** multi-series time line.
- **Source:** `rpt_artist_performance_daily`.
- **Dimension:** `chart_date`.
- **Breakdown dimension:** `artist_name`.
- **Metric:** `MAX(market_share)`.
- **Series limit:** five artists, selected by total streams in the report period.
- **Sort:** `chart_date` ascending.

**Business question:** Which artists are gaining or losing attention, and are leadership
changes sustained?

**Meaning:** each line is the artist's share of total Top 200 streams on a given day.
Parallel movement can indicate a market-wide event, while one artist's isolated jump is
more likely release-specific. The screenshot shows a temporary ANNA spike around
mid-July; the following days determine whether it was a launch peak or a durable gain.
Missing dates must be gaps rather than zero-share points.

### Longevità vs Intensità

- **Chart type:** bubble scatter plot.
- **Source:** `rpt_track_opportunities_daily`.
- **Dimension:** `chart_track_id`, with `track_name` and
  `artist_names_text` in the tooltip.
- **X-axis:** `days_on_chart`.
- **Y-axis:** `streams`.
- **Bubble size:** `movement_size`.
- **Optional colour dimension:** `release_stage`.
- **Date behavior:** latest snapshot only.

**Business question:** Which tracks combine lasting chart presence with strong current
consumption, and which new tracks have breakout intensity?

**Meaning:** upper-left tracks are new and intense, so they are launch or acceleration
candidates. Upper-right tracks combine longevity and high current demand and are
evergreen assets. Lower-right tracks are durable but lower-intensity catalogue; they
may still merit efficient maintenance. Bubble size highlights large absolute movements
that deserve investigation.

## Reading the current snapshot

The screenshots supplied for the report show data through **20 July 2026**. They are a
point-in-time illustration; the live report changes after successful scheduled runs.

- Average daily Top 200 streams are approximately **16.8 million**.
- Average daily stream change is approximately **−126 thousand**.
- Top 10 tracks account for approximately **14.05%** of streams; Top 50 account for
  **43.88%**.
- Collaborations represent approximately **43.08%** of charted tracks.
- Explicit tracks represent approximately **34.41%**.
- The artist bubble plot suggests a positive relationship between catalogue breadth and
  market share, with a dense long tail below roughly 1% share.
- The longevity plot shows very high intensity concentrated among newer tracks, while a
  smaller group sustains meaningful streams for years.

These observations are descriptive, not causal. Release calendars, missing snapshots
and artist collaborations can change the apparent relationship and should be checked
before allocating budget.
